Built for researchers

From numbers
to evidence.

Designed around the way research figures actually work: precise values, clear comparisons, restrained styling and control over the final composition.

01

Paste data. Start immediately.

Drop in CSV or JSON. VizLab detects fields and gives you chart-specific controls instead of making you configure a plotting library.

02

Choose the visual logic.

Trends, comparisons, composition, relationships and matrices each get their own visualization pattern.

03

Make dense figures readable.

Smart label placement, font controls, zoom, collision handling and movable legends help keep close values legible.

04

Style without the noise.

Editorial palettes, academic serif typography, clean grids and restrained visual hierarchy are ready from the start.

05

Export the figure.

Generate SVG for crisp publication workflows or high-resolution PNG for slides, documents and presentations.

06

Keep your data local.

The visualization maker is browser-based. Your data does not need to be sent to a visualization server.
